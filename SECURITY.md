# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/bthanhtung/snap-frame/security/advisories/new)
rather than in a public issue. Include the affected commit or the live URL,
steps to reproduce, and the impact. You will get an acknowledgement within a few
days, and a fix or mitigation plan once the report is confirmed.

## How SnapFrame handles your photos

- **Everything runs locally.** Decoding, colour grading, framing, and export
  happen in your browser. Photos and their EXIF data are never uploaded.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** The app has no backend, stores nothing about you,
  and sends no analytics.
- **Offline.** Once installed as a web app, SnapFrame works without a network.
- **Sharing** uses the browser's share sheet; the file goes only to the app you pick.
